# User guide by role

Common to all: the left sidebar shows only what your role may use; the top bar has search, the light/dark switch, notifications and your account. Phone numbers and National IDs appear masked (07** **** 45) unless your role may see them. After 10 minutes without activity you are signed out; a warning appears at 9 minutes with a **Stay signed in** button. On phones the sidebar opens from the menu button.

## Cashier
* **Cashier > My till**: open the till with your float. Take a deposit from the member's account page (Deposit), or loan repayments from the loan page (Repay).
* Withdrawals above the approval limit wait in the Approvals queue.
* Print a receipt from any transaction reference.
* **Close till**: type how many of each note/coin you hold. The page shows expected cash and the difference live. If there is a difference, write the reason.

## Loan officer
* Members > register a member (consent box required, National ID checked for format and duplicates, photo and KYC documents optional).
* Loans > Apply: pick the member and product, see the repayment preview, add guarantors, then review applications waiting for you. You cannot approve your own applications.

## Credit committee
Open an application from your notifications or Loans; read the member's savings, guarantors and history; Approve or Reject with a comment.

## General manager
Final loan approval, approvals queue, tills overview, reports, user list, audit trail and the dashboard.

## Accountant
Disburse approved loans, post income/expense vouchers (large expenses go for second approval), manual journals (always second approval), bank reconciliation, loan-loss provisioning, financial statements, reversals (approval required).

## Internal auditor
Read-only access to ledgers, reports (with export), tills, approvals and the audit trail, including login history. The auditor cannot change data.

## System administrator
Users, settings, approval limits, modules, maintenance, backups, integrations, member-portal PINs. See ADMIN_GUIDE.md.

## Members (portal)
Members sign in at `/portal/` with member number and a one-time PIN issued in person; they choose their own PIN at first sign-in. They can view balances, loan schedules and print statements. Staff can preview the USSD menu under "Member portal & USSD" in the sidebar. No USSD gateway is connected.
